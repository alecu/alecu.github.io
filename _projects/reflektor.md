You have to precisely align some mirrors so a laser reaches the goal, but only after solving puzzles and avoiding traps, and before time runs out. It's inspired by Costa Panayi's Deflektor, which I played in the eighties on a Czerweny CZ Spectrum, an Argentine ZX Spectrum clone.

Reflektor uses an Arduino for the game logic, another Arduino with an Adafruit Wave Shield for the music, and a custom board with 74HC595 and L293D chips to drive stepper motors salvaged from old floppy drives and printers. The controls and speaker come from a gutted radio. It also has a green laser, a smoke machine, plywood, and a lot of pre-1960s red and green Meccano.

It has been shown at the Museo del Juguete in San Isidro, Buenos Aires, among other places. You can [read the original post and watch the video](/arduino/electromechanical/games/reflektor/2013/09/22/reflektor/).
